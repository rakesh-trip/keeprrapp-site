FAQ

  • How do I upload a receipt? Tap + → Take Photo or Choose from Library. PDF import also supported.
  • Free limits & reward ads? Lifetime upload cap. Hit it? Watch Ad for one more, or upgrade to Pro.
  • Delete or restore receipts? Delete in-app → Recently Deleted (30 days). Restore from Settings → Recently Deleted.
  • Notifications? Local alerts for return/warranty deadlines. Enable in iOS Settings.
