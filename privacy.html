Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2025. Your privacy matters to us.

Information We Collect

  • Receipt data: Images you upload, extracted text (merchant, date, total, items), and any metadata we derive from your receipts.
  • Account data: Device identifiers, subscription status (Pro/Free), and usage-related data stored on your device and our servers.
  • Usage data: App interactions, feature usage, and analytics events (e.g., uploads, views) to improve the service.

How We Use Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide receipt storage, OCR extraction, and return/warranty tracking
  • Send local notifications for return and warranty deadlines
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: Displays advertisements (including rewarded ads for free users). AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data. You can limit ad tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • OCR Provider: Receipt images are processed by our backend to extract text and structure. Processing is performed server-side under our data handling policies.
  • Analytics: We use analytics to understand app usage and improve features. Data is aggregated and does not identify individual users.

Your Rights

  • Delete receipts: You can delete any receipt from the app. Deleted receipts move to Recently Deleted for 30 days, after which they are permanently removed.
  • Restore receipts: Within 30 days of deletion, you can restore receipts from Settings → Recently Deleted.
  • Request data deletion: To request deletion of all your data, email team@keeprrapp.com. We will process requests within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at team@keeprrapp.com.
