Free vs Pro Usage

  • Free: Limited number of receipts per lifetime (as displayed in-app). Free users may watch rewarded ads to unlock additional upload slots. Some features (e.g., viewing original receipt images) may be Pro-only.
  • Pro: Unlimited receipt uploads, no ads, and access to all features including priority processing and export.

Lifetime Upload Count

Free users have a lifetime upload limit. Once reached, you can either watch a rewarded ad to add one more receipt or upgrade to Pro for unlimited uploads. The limit is tied to your device and cannot be reset.

Delete and Restore Rules

  • Deleted receipts are moved to Recently Deleted for 30 days.
  • You can restore receipts from Recently Deleted at any time during that period.
  • After 30 days, receipts are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

Subscriptions & Billing

Pro is a paid subscription billed through your Apple ID. Payment is charged at confirmation. Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Refunds are subject to Apple's policies.
